    01 / Setup

    Start with your course page.

    1. 01

      Open WeBWorK

      Go to your school’s WeBWorK site and open the extension from your browser toolbar.

    2. 02

      Enable the extension

      Turn on Enable On This Site, or use Enable Globally for all WeBWorK sites.

    3. 03

      Refresh the page

      Your previews and tools will appear beside the problem. Change individual features below.

    02 / Tools

    Tools for your problem set.

    ƒ

    Read your answer as math

    Preview expressions as you type, check matching parentheses, and resize fields for longer answers.

    x =

    Use the problem’s variables

    Use detected values or define your own. Press Ctrl + Enter in an answer field to substitute them.

    ∫

    Keep a calculator open

    Open Desmos Scientific or Graphing in the side panel without leaving your assignment.

    [ ]

    Capture the question

    Copy or download a screenshot of the question. Headers and floating controls stay out of the image.

    Study mode / Optional

    Practice old problems again.

    Hide previous answers, correctness feedback, scores, and solutions. Peek when you’re ready to check. Your new answers are preserved.

    Peek / hide again
    Alt + Shift + P
    Study mode on / off
    Alt + Shift + H

    03 / Settings

    Keep what you use.

    Changes save automatically

    Refresh your WeBWorK page after changing these settings. Study mode updates immediately; its detailed controls are in the toolbar popup.
